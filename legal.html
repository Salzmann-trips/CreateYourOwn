Disclaimer

Trips on this site are created automatically with AI (Claude by Anthropic) from the answers you give. They are suggestions and inspiration, not bookings, not professional travel advice and not guaranteed to be correct or current. Check opening times, prices, tickets, road and weather conditions, ferries, seasonal closures and entry, visa, health and driving-licence rules with official sources before you travel. Drive times are estimates. You use the plans at your own risk; no liability is accepted for their accuracy or for decisions based on them. Links to Booking.com, Google Maps, Waze, Apple Maps, Wikipedia and other sites lead to third-party services with their own terms.

Privacy (draft)

  • No account, no tracking. The site doesn't use cookies for tracking or advertising.
  • Your trips are stored only in your own browser (local storage) until you delete them. A shared link contains the whole trip; anyone with the link can see it.
  • Creating a trip sends your wizard answers to our planning server and to Anthropic (Claude) to write the plan. Don't enter personal data in the free-text fields.
  • Third-party services receive your IP address when the page loads: GitHub Pages (hosting), Cloudflare (planning server and robot check), OpenStreetMap (map tiles), the OSRM routing server (drive times), cdnjs (map library) and Google Fonts (fonts).
